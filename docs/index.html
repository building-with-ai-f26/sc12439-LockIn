PRODUCT PAGE · PRD v1

LOCK-IN PARTY

An 8-bit party board game where friends lock in a 90-day goal, check in each week, and race each other around the board.

90 DAYS FRIENDS ONLY ANY GOAL

▶ THE PROBLEM

Friend groups start challenges all the time ("let's all work out 3x a week"), but they live in a group chat. After a week or two the messages stop, nobody knows who's still going, and there's no fun reason to keep showing up.

Lock-In Party turns a shared goal into a game: every check-in moves your piece on a retro board, so progress is visible, a little competitive, and actually fun to come back to.

▶ REACHABLE USER

Me and my friend group (5–6 people) who already talk in a group chat and have tried fitness challenges before. Several of us track workouts with Whoop; others want non-fitness goals like reading.

I can reach every one of them today by text, which means I can test with real people from week one.

▶ BEFORE / AFTER

Before

  • Someone proposes a challenge in the group chat.
  • Everyone agrees, but goals are vague.
  • Updates are random screenshots, if any.
  • By week 2 the chat goes quiet and the challenge quietly dies.

After

  • The group creates one 90-day challenge.
  • Each person locks in a specific goal on day 1.
  • Weekly check-ins move your piece around the board.
  • Everyone can see who's ahead, who's slipping, and what's at stake.

▶ CORE WORKFLOW

  1. Join a challenge. Open the group's board from an invite link.
  2. Lock in your goal. Write it once, specifically (e.g. "Work out 3x per week" or "Read 20 pages a day"). It can't be edited after lock-in.
  3. Check in. Each week, report whether you hit your goal.
  4. Move on the board. Hitting your goal moves your piece forward; missing it costs you spaces.
  5. See the standings. The board shows everyone's position and streaks through day 90.

▶ NON-GOALS (v1)

  • ✕No Whoop or wearable integration. Check-ins are self-reported for now. Auto-verifying fitness goals through Whoop is a later build.
  • ✕No minigames. Moving around the board is the whole game in v1; party-style minigames come later.
  • ✕No public matchmaking. Invite-only friend groups. No strangers, no global leaderboards.

▶ OPEN QUESTION

How do we verify non-fitness goals? Options: honor system, photo proof, or a friend approving each check-in. v1 uses the honor system; I'll watch whether my friends trust it.

▶ PROTOTYPE

8-Bit Me. Upload a photo and it becomes your game piece for the board: a pixel-art character snapped to a retro palette. Runs entirely in the browser, nothing is uploaded.
